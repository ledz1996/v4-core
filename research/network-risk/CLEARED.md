# Checks that came out safe

158 checks after cycle 1. Each entry gives what was tested, why it is safe, and the evidence.

## Arbitrum Nitro and Orbit chains

- Arbitrum One, Robinhood Chain, cycle 1. A1: whether an unlock flag, a currency delta or a synced currency can survive from one transaction into another through Nitro's extra transaction types (deposits, internal transactions, retryable submission, auto-redeems, manual redeems). Result: The state transition runs the ArbOS start hook and then StateDB.Prepare, which replaces the transient storage map, before any contract code runs. Deposit, internal and submit-retryable transactions end inside the hook and execute no contract code. Each scheduled redeem is appended to a queue and applied as its own transaction. Lock.sol:10-27, CurrencyDelta.sol:28-41, CurrencyReserves.sol:15-38 and NonzeroDeltaCount.sol:12-34 therefore see one transaction as on Ethereum. The go-ethereum file was read on the fork's master branch, which may differ from the commit pinned by the release. Evidence: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OffchainLabs/go-ethereum/master/core/state_transition.go and https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OffchainLabs/nitro/v3.11.3/arbos/block_processor.go
- Arbitrum One, Robinhood Chain, Elysium, cycle 1. A3 and A4: whether any ArbOS precompile can serve as an ERC-20 face of the native asset or as a currency whose transfer succeeds without moving anything. Result: balanceOf(address) and transfer(address,uint256) revert on every precompile address from 0x64 to 0x74 and on 0xff when called from the PoolManager address. sync calls balanceOf (PoolManager.sol:285, Currency.sol:94) and reverts, so no delta can ever be credited in such a currency, and a take reverts in Currency.sol:64-86. ArbInfo.getBalance exposes native balances under a different selector that the PoolManager never calls. Evidence: eth_call on https://arb1.arbitrum.io/rpc, https://rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com and https://rpc.elysium.kinetiq.xyz, queried 2026-11-22
- Arbitrum One, Robinhood Chain, cycle 1. A3: native balance credited to the PoolManager without a call, through a deposit transaction or a retryable refund address chosen by a parent chain sender. Result: Native settlement credits msg.value only (PoolManager.sol:353-354) and sync of the native currency records no balance (PoolManager.sol:281-283). A forced credit raises address(this).balance and is credited to nobody. It cannot be claimed and it cannot make a settle revert. Evidence: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OffchainLabs/nitro/v3.11.3/arbos/tx_processor.go

- all 21 networks, cycle 1. Whether sync, which runs while the manager is locked and whose result survives after unlock returns, lets one party collect a credit for tokens another party paid, under the rule that transient storage is cleared at the end of each transaction. Result: After unlock returns, the lock flag is false and every delta is zero. The synced currency and reserves are the only transient values that can remain. A credit from settle is the balance increase since the most recent sync of that currency (PoolManager.sol:358-360), sync overwrites both slots (286) and an ERC-20 settle resets the currency (361). A payer that syncs immediately before its transfer and settles immediately after leaves no window for another party. A stale sync by an earlier party can only make a later native settle with value revert (356) or make the later party's own sync replace it. The balance can also not fall between sync and settle through fee collection of that ERC-20 (ProtocolFees.sol:49-52), and a take in between makes settle revert on the checked subtraction (360). This holds only while transient storage is cleared between transactions (EIP-1153). If a network kept transient storage across transactions, a party could sync a token, wait, and later settle the whole net inflow since that sync; see the lead on this. - all 21 networks, cycle 1. The function lowers protocolFeesAccrued and the balance by the same amount and sends the tokens to the recipient the controller names. It changes no delta.
